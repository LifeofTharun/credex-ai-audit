# Success Metrics

## 🌟 The North Star Metric
**Total Identified Monthly Savings**: The aggregate dollar amount that our engine has identified as "savable" for our users. This metric directly tracks the value we are delivering to the ecosystem.

## 📈 Input Metrics (The Funnel)
1. **Audit Completion Rate**: % of users who land on the tool and finish the input form. (Target: >40%).
2. **"Strategic" Agreement Rate**: % of users who click "This is helpful" on the AI-generated strategic recommendation. (Target: >70%).
3. **Lead Qualification Rate**: % of audits that result in a "Credex Credit Eligible" status (Spend >$500/mo). (Target: 15-20%).
4. **Resend Report Open Rate**: % of users who open the audit report delivered to their inbox. (Target: >60%).

## 🛠️ Technical Health Metrics
- **Engine Latency**: Time from "Submit" to "Results Display" (Target: <3 seconds).
- **AI Fallback Rate**: % of audits where the OpenAI call fails and we fall back to static logic. (Target: <2%).
- **Database Uptime**: Supabase availability for storing audit logs.

## Month 1 Goals
- **500 Total Audits** completed.
- **$10,000 in Total Savings** identified for users.
- **50 Founders** converted into the Credex lead pipeline.
