# Project Economics & Unit Analysis

## The "Lead Gen" Engine
The primary economic function of the Audit Engine is to serve as a low-cost, high-scale acquisition channel for Credex's core financial products. Instead of paying $50-$100 per lead via Google Ads, the Audit Engine creates a proprietary data stream of high-intent customers.

## Unit Economics (Per Audit)
To understand the profitability, we must look at the cost of running a single audit vs. the potential revenue from a converted lead.

### 1. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- **API Costs (OpenAI GPT-4o)**: ~$0.05 per audit (optimized via JSON mode and caching).
- **Email Delivery (Resend)**: $0.00 (free tier) or ~$0.01 at scale.
- **Hosting (Supabase/Vercel)**: ~$0.02 (pro-rated across users).
- **Total Variable Cost**: **~$0.08 per audit**.

### 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Since the tool is distributed organically via founder communities and "Build in Public" content, the initial CAC is primarily "Time." However, at scale, we estimate a blended CAC (including distribution efforts) of **$2.00 per user**.

## Conversion Math & Revenue Projections
Let's analyze a sample of 1,000 audits performed by startup founders.

### Funnel Metrics
- **Audits Performed**: 1,000
- **Qualification Rate (Spend > $500/mo)**: 20% (200 users)
- **Click-Through to "Credex Credits"**: 30% of qualified users (60 users)
- **Final Conversion to Paid/Managed Product**: 10% of qualified users (20 users)

### Lifetime Value (LTV) Calculation
When a user converts to a Credex Managed Product or uses Credex Credits:
- **Revenue per Managed Client**: ~$100/mo management fee or % of savings.
- **LTV (12-month horizon)**: ~$1,200.

### The "Credex Delta"
For the 20 users who convert:
- **Total Revenue**: 20 * $1,200 = **$24,000**.
- **Total Cost of Funnel**: 1,000 audits * $0.08 = $80.
- **Total CAC**: 1,000 * $2.00 = $2,000.
- **ROI**: ($24,000 / $2,080) = **~11.5x Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**.

## Strategic Economic Value: Data as an Asset
Beyond the direct revenue, the Audit Engine provides Credex with a unique **Pricing Proprietary Dataset**. 
1. **Market Benchmarking**: By aggregating anonymized data, Credex can tell a user exactly how much their peers are paying for Enterprise ChatGPT. This "Information Asymmetry" is a powerful moat that traditional banks don't have.
2. **Predictive Churn**: If a company starts aggressively auditing their AI spend, it's a signal they are tightening their belt. Credex can proactively reach out with debt financing or credit solutions before the company runs out of cash.

## Conclusion
The economics of the Audit Engine are highly asymmetric. By spending pennies on API calls, Credex generates high-trust leads that would otherwise cost thousands in traditional marketing. The "Tool-as-a-Service" model transforms our marketing from an expense into a value-added utility for the customer, drastically increasing the LTV-to-CAC ratio.
