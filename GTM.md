# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## The Problem Space
In the current "AI Gold Rush," startups are subscribing to every new tool that promises a productivity boost. However, most founders have lost track of their total AI burn. They have 12 developers on Cursor, but forgot to cancel their 12 GitHub Copilot seats. They have a "ChatGPT Team" plan for 3 people, even though they only use 1. This "Subscription Leakage" is a silent killer of runway.

## Target Audience: The "Burn-Conscious" Founder
Our primary target is the Series A startup founder or CTO. These teams are large enough (10-50 people) to have significant subscription bloat but small enough that they don't have a dedicated procurement or FinOps department. They are currently looking for ways to extend their runway without cutting headcount.

## Acquisition Strategy: The "Audit-First" Hook
Our acquisition strategy is built around a "Lead Magnet" utility: The Free AI Spend Audit.

### 1. Community Distribution (Days 1-15)
We will launch the audit tool as a free resource in high-density founder communities:
- **YC Bookface & Indie Hackers**: Post a "Free Tool" announcement showing a screenshot of a $500/mo savings report.
- **LinkedIn/Twitter "Burn Audit" Series**: I will publish weekly "AI Spend Audits" of hypothetical (or anonymized) companies. For example: "How we found $6k/year in savings for a 20-person dev shop."
- **Niche Discord Servers**: Target servers like "Latent Space" or "AI Engineer" where early adopters congregate.

### 2. The "FOMO" Referral Loop
On the results page of the audit, we include a "Compare Your Score" feature. Users can see where they stand against other startups of their size (e.g., "You are in the top 10% of AI spenders for a 15-person team"). This encourages founders to share their "Optimization Score" on social media, creating organic viral loops.

### 3. Direct Cold Outreach (The "CFO" Angle)
We will use automated tools to find startups that have recently announced "Scaling" or "Hiring." We will reach out to their Finance Leads with a simple value prop: "We built a 30-second audit that finds an average of 15% savings on AI stacks. Want to see your report?"

## Conversion & Monetization
The Audit Tool is free, but the "Fix" is where Credex monetizes. 

- **Tier 1: The Credits Path**: If the user is spending >$500/mo, we offer them a path to apply for **Credex Credits**. This offsets their existing spend and locks them into the Credex ecosystem.
- **Tier 2: Managed Optimization**: For Enterprise teams (spend >$2k/mo), we offer a "Managed Procurement" service where Credex takes a % of the total savings achieved.

## Launch Timeline
- **Week 1**: Beta launch to 10 friendly founders for feedback.
- **Week 2**: Public launch on Product Hunt as a "Free Utility."
- **Week 3**: Cold outreach campaign targeting 200 Series A startups.
- **Week 4**: Evaluate conversion metrics and refine the "Strategic Recommendations" AI prompt based on real user data.

## Conclusion
By positioning the Audit Engine as a high-value, low-friction utility, we build trust with founders immediately. We aren't just "another tool"—we are the tool that pays for itself by finding waste in their existing stack. This "Value-In-Advance" model is the most effective way to enter the crowded AI market.
